Two-factor authentication code generator (TOTP)

Keys are stored in this browser on this computer (localStorage) and are never sent anywhere.
A different browser, a different computer, or clearing browsing data means the keys will not be there. Keep a backup of your keys (button at the bottom).
Backup: the button below prints all your keys as plain text — copy them into a file and keep it safe. You can paste that same text here on another computer and press Load.
